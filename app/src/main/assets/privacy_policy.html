CredStow Privacy Policy Effective: October 2026

CredStow (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an Android application that securely stores and optionally validates your AI API keys — such as those for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and others — entirely on your device.

This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is protected, and with whom it is (or is not) shared. Our guiding principle is simple: your API keys and their metadata never leave your device unless you explicitly initiate an action that requires it.

No account or registration is required. There is no sign-up, no login, and no server-side account. All data lives on the device you install the app on.

1. Information the App Stores On Your Device

API keys (the core secret)

  • When you add a key, you paste the plaintext API key (e.g. sk-… for OpenAI). The app encrypts it immediately and never stores the plaintext.
  • Encryption is AES-256-GCM with a randomly generated 256-bit Data-Encryption Key (DEK). The DEK itself is stored in Android’s EncryptedSharedPreferences, which wraps it in the Android Keystore system (a hardware-backed or strongBox-protected master key). Two layers of at-rest encryption.
  • A non-secret mask preview (last four characters) is also stored so the key list can display a readable identifier without decrypting anything.
  • The plaintext key is recovered from the encrypted blob only in transient memory, for three user-initiated actions: copy-to-clipboard, the test ping (Pro only), and backup export. It is never persisted in plaintext, never written to logs, and never sent to our servers.

Key metadata

The following non-secret metadata is stored in a local Room database:

  • Label and optional notes you assign to each key
  • Provider identity (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.)
  • Key status (Untested, Testing, Valid, Invalid, Error) — the result of the last test ping
  • Creation and last-updated timestamps

Your settings

All preferences are stored locally in encrypted DataStore:

  • Clipboard auto-clear interval (5–120 seconds)
  • Biometric lock toggle
  • Font-size scale preference
  • Onboarding completion state
  • Ad-interstitial cadence state (free tier only)
  • The set of product IDs you have purchased (the Pro status flag)

2. Information Sent Over the Network

The app does not upload your keys, metadata, or usage data to any server we control. No analytics, no crash reports, and no telemetry are sent to the developer. The only outbound network traffic initiated by the app falls into two categories, both of which are user-driven:

(a) Key test ping — Pro feature, fully user-initiated

When you are a Pro user and tap the “Test key” button on a key’s detail screen, the app:

  • Decrypts the key in transient memory
  • Constructs a minimal authenticated GET request to that provider’s official API endpoint (e.g. https://api.openai.com/v1/models for OpenAI, https://api.anthropic.com/v1/models for Anthropic)
  • Sends the key as a standard Authorization header (Bearer token for most providers, API-key parameter for Google) over HTTPS/TLS
  • Reads only the HTTP status code (2xx = valid, 401/403 = invalid, other = error) and immediately discards the response body
  • Never logs the key or the response content

This request goes directly to the AI provider you selected. It is not routed through, proxied by, or visible to the CredStow developer. Azure OpenAI and “Other” providers do not support testing and will never trigger a network request.

By using Test key, you authorize the app to transmit your decrypted API key to the provider’s public API for the sole purpose of validating it. Standard provider rate limits and terms apply.

(b) Google services — billing and advertising

  • Google Play Billing: When you upgrade to Pro, the app communicates with Google Play to process the one-time purchase (“pro_unlock”) and validate your ownership status. Google handles payment and purchase data per their terms. Your purchase state is cached locally in encrypted DataStore for fast UI reads.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Free (non-Pro) users see a banner ad and occasional interstitials at certain milestones. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your AAID or a resettable ad ID) and use cookies per Google’s Ads Privacy & Terms. Pro users never see ads or send any ad-related data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Because data never leaves your device, usage is strictly local:

  • To display your keys, their masked previews, and their test status
  • To decrypt keys only when you explicitly copy, test, or export them
  • To encrypt / decrypt keys with the Keystore-backed DEK for storage and retrieval
  • To process Pro upgrades via Google Play Billing
  • (Free tier only) To load banner and interstitial ads via the Google Mobile Ads SDK

4. Your Rights and Controls

Delete a single key Tap Delete on the key’s detail screen. It is removed from the database and Room in seconds.
Delete all keys Uninstalling the app removes all app data (keys, metadata, settings) from the device, as Google Play/Play Store manages uninstall cleanup.
Export an encrypted backup (Pro) Generate a password-protected, AES-256-GCM-encrypted backup of your keys and paste it into a file of your choice for safekeeping.
Import a backup (Pro) Restore keys from a previously exported encrypted backup blob.
Opt out of ads Upgrade to Pro. No ad-related data is ever collected from Pro users.
Disable biometric lock Toggle it off in Settings at any time. The app will then open directly to your key list.
Reset advertising ID Control ad tracking via your device’s Settings → Google → Ads menu.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

CredStow retains your data indefinitely, on-device only, until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry, no server-side account to delete, and no data sync across devices. Deleting the app (or clearing its storage in Android Settings) permanently erases all keys and metadata irreversibly — the encryption keys are likewise destroyed, making recovery computationally impossible.

We cannot recover deleted keys or backups, so export an encrypted backup before uninstalling if you want a copy.

6. Security Practices

  • Two-layer encryption at rest: API key plaintext is encrypted with AES-256-GCM (random 96-bit nonce per key) using a 256-bit DEK. The DEK is stored in EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256-GCM / AES-256-SIV), whose master key is managed by the Android Keystore (hardware-backed where available).
  • No key in logs: StrictCode paths that decrypt keys never log them. The OkHttp client used for test pings is built with connectTimeout(15s) / readTimeout(15s) and carries no logging interceptor.
  • Defensive decryption: Tampered ciphertext or wrong keys fail GCM authentication and are rejected (the decrypt call returns null), never surfacing partial or corrupted secrets.
  • Clipboard auto-clear: Copied keys are wiped from the clipboard after a user-chosen interval (default 30 seconds).
  • Pro guarded: Sensitive actions (test ping, backup export, import) check Pro ownership at the use-case level before executing.

7. Children’s Privacy

CredStow is not directed at children and is not marketed to anyone under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child’s data has been provided through the app, contact us and we will remove it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored entirely on your device and is not transferred across borders by CredStow. Any network requests during a test ping go to the provider’s infrastructure, which may be located in various jurisdictions — this is governed by that provider’s own privacy policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, the effective date above will change. Substantive changes that affect your rights will be communicated via an in-app notice and a Play Store release-notes update.

10. Analytics and Crash Reporting

As of this writing, CredStow does not integrate any third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We collect no usage metrics, no device identifiers, and no crash traces. If this changes in a future release, we will update this policy and provide an in-app opt-out where required.
